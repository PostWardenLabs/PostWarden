Account levels

Names for each step down the chart of accounts — depth 1 is every account's root (Assets, Liabilities, ...), depth 2 their children, and so on, however deep your chart goes. Purely labels: the hierarchy itself is still just accounts and their parent. Pick one of these as a scenario's base level to let it post to a whole branch — "Bank" — instead of every leaf underneath it.
